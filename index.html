Frequently asked

Questions people ask before booking

Who is this for?
Creators teaching AI engineering, machine learning, data science or data careers on YouTube, who also run a business alongside the channel: a course, a cohort, a paid community, coaching or consulting. You're already publishing or ready to start, and you're in it for the long game, not a single viral hit.
Do you understand the technical content?
Well enough to edit it without breaking it. I build AI workflows myself and teach professionals how to use Claude, so I follow what you're explaining and won't cut the sentence that carries the point. You review every edit, and anything I'm unsure about gets flagged, not guessed.
What do I need to send you?
Raw footage and any screen recordings, plus whatever you wrote first: an outline, a script or a blog post. That draft is where the graphics come from. Upload to a shared drive and we handle structure, pacing, and delivery from there.
How does the trial edit work?
Every collaboration starts with a single trial edit. You see exactly how we structure, cut, and pace before committing to anything ongoing. If it works, we continue. If not, you still walk away with a finished video.
How do revisions work?
We use Frame.io for all reviews. Timestamped, centralized, no long email threads. One round of revisions is included before you post.
Can you match my existing brand style?
Yes. The Channel Blueprint locks in your visual identity before we cut anything. Every edit after that applies it consistently, so you don't have to brief us every time.
How does billing work?
Monthly subscription, billed at the start of each month. Cancel anytime with one month's notice.

The background behind the editing.

Most editors come from post-production. My background is different, and I think it shows in the work. I have a Master's in Film Directing from the University of Edinburgh, spent fourteen years teaching film and communication at universities in Kyoto and Osaka, Japan, and worked in VFX production at DNEG on Blade Runner 2049.

Teaching shaped how I think about structure. Film shaped how I think about audience attention. These days I also build AI workflows and teach professionals to use Claude, so I come to your material understanding what it's for. Based in Calgary, Alberta.

Sidney's Catalogue is a Philip K. Dick reference. That's intentional.
