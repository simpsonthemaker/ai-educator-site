Frequently asked

Questions teams ask before booking

Our team isn't technical. Is that a problem?
No. That is who this is built for. Levels 1 and 2 need no technical background, and Level 3 is optional.
Which Claude plan do we need?
Most teams use Claude Team or Enterprise. If you are still choosing, the first call covers what fits your size and security needs.
How do you handle sensitive data?
Safe use is part of Level 1, and every session follows your company's own AI and data policy. If you don't have one yet, we start there.
Do you build for us or teach us?
Both, in that order. Workflows are built with your team, on their work, so they can run and change them after I leave.
Remote or on-site?
Remote across North America. On-site in Calgary and the surrounding area.
